# Operating expense guidance

For an operating expense comparison, confirm that base and current periods
are complete and comparable, that amounts follow the organization's expense
sign convention, and that account and cost-center fields are available.
Group movements by the dimensions the analyst chooses, keep the remainder
reconciled, and use source descriptions only as supporting drill evidence.

The preset's column names and monthly comparison are starting points. They
must be mapped and confirmed for each new file. Ask before adding account
filters, entity exclusions, or a favorable/unfavorable interpretation.
