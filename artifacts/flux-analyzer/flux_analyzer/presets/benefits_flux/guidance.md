# Employee benefits guidance

Compare like-for-like periods and explicitly choose the Actual and Forecast
scenarios. Use the department dimension to show the population behind the
movement. Cost per head is a supplementary, non-additive rate; do not use it
as the driver total.

Volume-at-base-rate separates the effect of headcount from the residual rate
effect and reconciles to total cost movement. Confirm that the source's
headcount and cost units align to the same time period and population.
Scenario labels and any employee categories must come from the uploaded data
and approved policy. Do not infer employee-level facts or send employee rows
to the agent.

The default department/scenario settings are illustrative. If a file is
annual, quarterly, or lacks both scenarios, ask the analyst to choose a
meaningful like-for-like comparison.
